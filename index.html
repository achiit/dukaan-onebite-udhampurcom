About OneBite

A cafe our own neighbourhood can afford.

I grew up around Shiv Nagar, and for years my friends and I had nowhere decent to sit, eat and talk without spending a lot. So in 2024, with my family backing me, I opened OneBite.

The idea was simple: good fast food, made fresh, at prices where you do not have to count your money. That is why our burgers start at Rs. 49. Most of our regulars are students, families and office-goers from nearby, and many now order for us by name.
